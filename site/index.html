Bonus parlay

For fun · not in the record

One pick'em entry a day when there are games, posted late morning: Saturday for Sunday's NFL games, and the same day for NBA games. It has at least three picks (what the apps' boost promos require), one per player, same game allowed. Each pick must be at least a 54% favorite at the app's line by the average of Pinnacle's and Kalshi's prices. At the normal payout that's usually still a losing bet, so each card shows the boost it needs; it's meant for days the app runs a promo. Posting hours before kickoff means injury news can still void a pick. Results are shown at the normal payout and, when a boost was running, at the boosted payout. None of it counts toward our record.

NBA Test

Paper only

We're testing NBA player props on paper before anything goes live. Since Oct 26 (rules v3) a pick needs an exchange price (Novig or ProphetX) whose break-even, after fees, is at least 3 points below Kalshi's price for the same side. At most 3 picks per game and 8 per night, biggest gaps first. Picks are judged on results: to pass, 400 graded picks must win at least 3 points more often than their break-even, with the 95% range above zero and a profit. Nothing here counts in the NFL record.

Oct 22–25 used rules v2 (Unders 3%+, Overs 6%+ against Pinnacle and Kalshi combined). We scored those against Kalshi's closing price, but Kalshi was part of the price we bet against and barely moves before tip-off, so that score mostly repeated the edge we started with. It said "passed"; the results over those nights (86-102, about what Kalshi's prices expected) don't show a proven edge. v2 bets are still tracked but no longer posted.

Our first rules (Oct 20–21) compared exchange prices with Pinnacle alone and went 17-39; retired Oct 22. The matchup test (past results against tonight's opponent, priced at Kalshi) keeps a separate record and is decided at 1,500 bets or Jan 31.

    How it works

    Price first

    We compare prices on betting exchanges (Novig, ProphetX, Kalshi) with Pinnacle, the sharpest sportsbook, after removing its built-in margin. A pick is posted only when the price you can actually get is at least 3% better than that fair price. Each card shows the worst price still worth taking.

    Timing

    Most picks go out on Sunday mornings, right after inactives are announced, when our research showed prices are softest. Breaking quarterback and injury news triggers extra checks during the week.

    Teasers

    Two-team, six-point teasers on underdogs of +1.5 to +2.5 in games with totals of 49 or less. Teasing through 3 and 7 has won about 78% of legs since 2014, above the 74% needed at the usual price.

    Grading

    Results come from official box scores. Closing-line value compares our price with the no-margin closing price at Pinnacle. Picks marked "paper" are tracked but not counted.

    NBA Test

    NBA picks are on paper while we test them. Every bet the model would make is posted and graded on the NBA Test tab, but none go to Discord or into the record. NBA bets are graded against Kalshi's price at tip-off, because Pinnacle's NBA prop prices rarely move before the game. Our first NBA rules failed this check and were retired on Oct 22 (17-39); their bets are still shown.

    Units

    Every stake and result is in units, so the record means the same thing whatever your bankroll. One unit is 2% of your bankroll. With a $500 bankroll, 1 unit is $10: a 0.5u pick is a $5 bet, a 0.75u Strong pick is $7.50, and a +1.2u week means you're up $12. Picks are flat 0.5u to 0.75u, and long shots (+150 or longer) stay at 0.5u.

    Receipts

    Every pick is committed to a public GitHub repository when it appears here, before kickoff, so the timestamps can be checked.

    Get picks when news breaks
